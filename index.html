Lab calendar assistant

An internal assistant for the Pilpel Lab. It keeps the lab's group-meeting calendar in sync with the lab schedule sheet, and sends the related notification email on the lab's behalf.

Reads the schedule

Reads the group-meeting schedule from the lab's Google Sheet, which stays the source of truth.

Updates the calendar

Applies speaker names and any time overrides to the matching events on the lab calendars.

Sends notifications

Sends scheduling and reminder email from the lab account when a change needs to go out.

This is a private, single-account tool. It has no public sign-up, and it acts only on the lab Google account that granted it access. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Access and data

The assistant uses Google Calendar and Gmail access granted by the lab account. What it reads, how it is stored, and how to revoke access are described in the privacy policy.
